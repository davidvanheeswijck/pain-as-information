# Intent: keep the repository open as an evidence resource only

Author: the programme owner
Status: accepted 21/11/2026
Area: programme
Source: Claude Code session, 21/11/2026 (also answered in the owner's decision ledger)

## In the owner's words
> "Keep it open as an evidence resource only." (21/11/2026), in reply to four options after the two-unscorable stop rule was met.

The four options, as they were put to the owner:

1. Close and write up.
2. Keep open as an evidence resource only: no further predictions or simulations; the briefs are updated when relevant papers appear.
3. Continue with a human route.
4. Pause.

The owner chose the second.

## Problem
The stop rule written into the P-003 registration was met. Two registrations
against the same unread paper ended unscorable for lack of a source: P-003
(`predictions/P-003-sham-pain-against-pre-implant/RESULT.md`, attempt 1 of 2)
and P-004
(`predictions/P-004-sham-pain-against-pre-implant-attempt-2/RESULT.md`,
attempt 2 of 2). `ledger/OPEN.md` records it under "What the programme does
next", step 2: "The stop rule is met", and "Nothing further is done here."
By the rule as written, the question of closing went to the owner. The
programme has made no prediction that was scored against unread data, and
under PROGRAMME.md's test it has no progress to show since D-003.

## Proposed outcome
1. **Keep the repository open as an evidence resource only.** Record it as
   decision D-004 in `ledger/DECISIONS.md`.
2. **No further predictions or simulations.** The repository registers no new
   prediction and runs no new simulation.
3. **Conjectures and panels are paused as a consequence.** The owner's words
   name predictions and simulations. They do not name conjectures or panels.
   The pause is an interpretation, made because D-003 already lists "a tenth
   conjecture filed only to trigger a review" under not-to-do, and because
   conjectures and panels served the closed hard core. It is flagged in D-004
   for the owner to confirm.
4. **The briefs are maintained.** They are corrected when an error is found and
   updated when a relevant paper appears, under the existing rules in
   EPISTEMICS.md. The refutation ledger and the harness stay as published.
5. **The review cycle is suspended** while D-004 stands, because there are no
   predictions or simulations for a progressive-or-degenerating review to
   assess. If work resumes under a later decision (D-005), the next review
   counts as the second.
6. **Bring README.md, PROGRAMME.md, `ledger/OPEN.md`, `evidence/README.md`, the
   dashboard text and the citation file into line**, with dated notes and no
   deletion of earlier text.

## Not to do
Any new prediction, simulation or evidence-brief content as part of this
change. A reversal without a written D-005 first.

## Constraints
- ETHICS.md is unchanged: no case data, no medical advice, honest failure is
  the deliverable.
- The refutation ledger, the evidence briefs and the harness are kept as they
  are.
- Earlier text is annotated, not deleted.
