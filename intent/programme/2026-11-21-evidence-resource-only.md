# Intent: keep the repository open as an evidence resource only

Author: the programme owner (the choice below); the session drafted the rest
Status: the owner's choice accepted 21/11/2026; the drafted items below are pending the owner's confirmation
Area: programme
Source: Claude Code session, 21/11/2026 (also answered in the owner's decision ledger)

## In the owner's words
> "Keep it open as an evidence resource only." (21/11/2026), in reply to four options after the two-unscorable stop rule was met.

The four options, as they were put to the owner:

1. Close and write up.
2. Keep open as an evidence resource only: no further predictions or simulations; the briefs are updated when relevant papers appear.
3. Continue with a human route.
4. Pause.

## What the owner decided
The owner chose option 2 as put: no further predictions or simulations; the
briefs are updated when relevant papers appear. Nothing else in this file is the
owner's decision.

## Problem
The stop rule written into the P-003 registration was met. Two registrations of
one prediction, against the same unread paper, ended unscorable for lack of a
source: P-003 (`predictions/P-003-sham-pain-against-pre-implant/RESULT.md`,
attempt 1 of 2) and P-004
(`predictions/P-004-sham-pain-against-pre-implant-attempt-2/RESULT.md`,
attempt 2 of 2). `ledger/OPEN.md` records it under "What the programme does
next", step 2: "The stop rule is met", and "Nothing further is done here."
By the rule as written, the question of closing went to the owner. No
prediction was scored against unread data, and under PROGRAMME.md's test the
programme has no progress to show since D-003.

## Proposed outcome
Recorded as decision D-004 in `ledger/DECISIONS.md`, with the same split.

1. **Decided by the owner:** the repository stays open as an evidence resource;
   no further predictions or simulations; the briefs are updated when relevant
   papers appear.
2. **Bring README.md, PROGRAMME.md, `ledger/OPEN.md`, `evidence/README.md`, the
   dashboard text and the citation file into line**, with dated notes and no
   deletion of earlier text.

## Drafted by the session, pending the owner's confirmation
These follow from the owner's words as the session read them. They are not
stated by the owner.

- **Conjectures and panels are paused.** The owner's words name predictions and
  simulations only. The pause is an interpretation, made because D-003 already
  lists "a tenth conjecture filed only to trigger a review" under not-to-do, and
  because conjectures and panels served the closed hard core.
- **The review cycle is suspended** while D-004 stands, because there are no
  predictions or simulations for a progressive-or-degenerating review to assess.
  The every-ten-pieces trigger is already at ten, so the suspension is the only
  thing that prevents a second review. If work resumes under a D-005, the next
  review counts as the second.
- **Reversal conditions and the D-005 requirement.** Reversing needs a new
  decision, D-005, written before any such work starts. Conditions: access to
  full texts by a declared human or institutional route, together with a
  registered prediction the owner approves; or a published, validated model that
  reproduces its own results here and bears on one of the three questions, as
  judged by the owner in D-005.
- **Maintenance detail beyond "briefs are updated".** Briefs are also corrected
  when an error is found, under the existing rules in EPISTEMICS.md; the
  refutation ledger and the harness stay as published.

## Not to do
Any new prediction, simulation or evidence-brief content as part of this
change. A reversal without a written D-005 first.

## Constraints
- ETHICS.md is unchanged: no case data, no medical advice, honest failure is
  the deliverable.
- The refutation ledger, the evidence briefs and the harness are kept as they
  are.
- Earlier text is annotated, not deleted.
